Genesis 2.0

Switzerland · 2018 · Documentary feature · Co-director, Cinematographer

The hunt for white gold at the remotest edge of Siberia reveals a scenario of the future that may well turn our world upside down.

On the remote New Siberian Islands in the Arctic Ocean, hunters search for tusks of extinct mammoths. One day, they discover a surprisingly well-preserved mammoth carcass. Resurrecting the woolly mammoth is a first manifestation of the next great technological revolution — genetics. It may well turn our world upside down.

Academy Award Nominee · Member of the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

Maxim Arbugaev was born in 1991 in Tiksi, a remote port town on the shore of the Arctic Ocean, at the northern edge of Siberia. He played professional ice hockey for 15 years before discovering his passion for filmmaking. Maxim’s films explore the relationship between people and nature in remote northern communities, combining observational documentary with a poetic visual language.

His work has screened at Berlinale, IDFA, Locarno, Visions du Réel and BFI London Film Festival, among others. He won the Sundance Award for Cinematography for Genesis 2.0, was nominated for an Academy Award with his sister Evgenia Arbugaeva for Haulout, and made his fiction debut with The Summer Will End. His latest work as cinematographer, Chuuraa, won the Grand Prix at the 34th CAMERIMAGE.
